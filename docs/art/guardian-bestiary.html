Guardian Bestiary

What the spawn are a miniature of. Six wardens grafted into the binding and decayed under it (lore §2): each is its mortal type's geometry at a Late's scale or past it, and wears the piece of the lock it carries — a ring in the Ancient hue with the lock's own eye in it. Break the seal and the finale fields the same figure with the ring gone.

Two eyes, wrong-placed, and a third in the seal. The seal's never blinks with the beast's.
The seal is worn. A collar, a brand, a crown — where it sits is the Guardian's identity.
Unsealed is derived. The same drawing, the ring off, a scar where it sat.
Every Guardian breaks the frame. The Endbringer breaks it on every edge.
The Endbringer is the seal's colour and wears five broken; the sixth chain runs off the frame.
