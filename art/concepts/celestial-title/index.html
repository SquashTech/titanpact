The Infinite, tapped

No words but the Cycle's own. The Cycles sit at the top: arrows step through them, the pips are the five, and gold is cleared. The view below takes the chosen Cycle's colour. Enter starts that Cycle directly, so the separate Which Cycle? screen could go.

  • Everything here is drawn in code (canvas), no new art: it scales to any phone and costs no PixelLab budget.
  • Strange Worlds, the Trials and the Gauntlet would each get their own treatment in the same spirit once a direction is picked.
  • Animations are slow on purpose: the title sits open for a long time, and a busy background tires the eye.
