The Reference, three ways

  • All three: one always-on Close at the bottom, where the thumb is; no ✕ hunt in a corner. A tap outside still closes A and B.
  • Tabs become six icons with short labels (How to Play, Types, Statuses, Passives, Fields, Gear), one row instead of two rows of words.
  • The stone frame goes. Entries are soft rows with their emblem, a chevron, and a gold rim when open.
  • Tap the tabs and the How to Play entries to feel the navigation. Other tabs show a placeholder.
