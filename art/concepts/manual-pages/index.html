The Manual Grows — the Clearing just won

What all three share

  • No lesson names. A woken star holds a blank page instead of a typed-in name; the next lesson is known by what it will add, not what it is called.
  • The page a lesson files is the general rule (How to Play's voice), not the in-fight tip. The fight says "Iron Warden has no Mana"; the page says what Rest is. One source, two voices: the situational line stays in the fight.
  • The Clearing teaches six things, so it files six pages. That is the honest count, and the main thing to judge here: six taps after a fight in A and B, one glance in C.
  • Pictures are stand-ins. The real pages would draw with the tip-scene renderer (tipScenes.ts), the same staged fights How to Play already uses.
  • Pages the lessons never teach (Gems, the Guild Hall, the Academy, Cycles…) would file quietly the first time a run meets them. Not shown here.

The open question each one answers differently

  • A shows only what you have. Nothing hints at the size of the book.
  • B shows the shelf with blank leaves still to fill. The pull of the next part, but it is a visible count, without a number.
  • C files pages under the stars that taught them. The dark stars are what is left, a count drawn as the sky you already know.
