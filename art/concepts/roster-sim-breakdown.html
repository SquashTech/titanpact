Roster sim breakdown: all 84

Sim pass 12, 2026-09-28: 40,000 runs (20,000 skilled pilot, 20,000 chart pilot) at shipped values, with every one of the 84 heroes owned and drafted on equal odds. Full clears: 82.9% skilled, 39.3% chart. Results are directional: they find what is mechanically out of line, and playtesting decides.

Headlines

  • The roster is spread about one fight wide. Most heroes sit within ±1 fight of draft lift. Ten are clear outliers: five strong (Ferra, Gallant, Bellows, Whirr, Ronin) and five weak (Kite, Patch, Mellow, Pixie, Morel). Evolution paths are at parity: all 249 measured paths land within ±0.7 of a fight, so no path is a trap.
  • Supports are the weakest group in the game. The eleven heroes dealing under 70 damage a round average about −1 fight of lift under both pilots. That covers the old supports (Pixie, Solace, Aegis) as much as the new (Patch, Mellow, Totem, Selkie, Hart, Kite). Part of it is the sim: its pilot values moves in HP, and it underrates speed control, buffs and cleanses. But draft lift measures whole runs, and the gap is too consistent to be only that. This is the biggest balance question the pass raises.
  • Physical beats magical beats mixed, the pattern every earlier pass found: physical +0.38 / +0.53, magical −0.27 / −0.31, mixed −0.52 / −0.96 (skilled / chart lift). The new heroes lean physical, which is part of why they measure stronger.
  • The new 32 are slightly stronger than the old 52 (+0.19 / +0.59 against −0.13 / −0.39). It's mild power creep, concentrated in Iron, Mech and Water attackers.
  • By type, Iron is strongest and Storm weakest. Iron averages +0.68 / +1.37, with all six heroes in the top 40. Storm is last (−0.61 / −1.53), then Nature, Frost and Light. Storm's Conduct engine is the most pilot-sensitive thing in the roster: Skyshear and Kite collapse under the chart pilot.
  • The new base roster has one real problem: Patch. Six of the eight promoted heroes measure on or above the curve (Ashwing ranks 9th). Patch is the second-weakest hero in the game, and Selkie is 73rd. Bellows, who left the base, is 3rd. See the Mech and Water notes.
  • Watch items from earlier, resolved. Abacus's Tally is above the curve (19th) but not broken. Koan's Satori does not make him strong: he ranks 66th, the most extreme late bloomer in the game (trade ratio 1.28 early, 4.22 late). Scree's thin Late pool doesn't hurt him; he ranks 21st.

What I'd do next

  1. Rethink the base Mech seat. Put Whirr (4th) or Abacus (19th) in for Patch, or keep Patch and buff him. Only Kite ranks lower.
  2. Take the support question on as a system, not hero by hero. Check first whether the pilot undervalues support moves, by pairing the playtest feel against the sim. Then decide whether healing and buff numbers need a pass.
  3. Kite needs a real rework. He ranks worst in the game, about 3.4 fights of lift down under both pilots. His Headwind only pays when he doesn't attack, and his kit leaves him with little to do.
  4. Storm needs a look after Kite: Skyshear at 75th, Tempest at 68th and Nimbus at 44th leave only Raiju and Squall healthy.
  5. Check the strong five for number trims (Ferra, Gallant, Bellows, Whirr, Ronin). A small trim is enough. Nothing breaks the game.

Method

  • Mode. SIM_ALL_HEROES=1 (new): owns the whole catalog and drafts one hero from each type's full six rather than the deck's three. Every hero also feeds contracts and the Guild Hall. Seed 84 for both batches.
  • Draft lift = encounters won after the draft by runs that took this hero, minus runs where it was offered and left. That's out of about 16 fights a run. n ≈ 450 drafts a hero; a standard error is about 0.25, so |lift| > 0.5 is real.
  • Trade ratio = damage dealt / damage taken per round on the field. It is role-biased: a healer or buffer scores low by construction, so the power score weights it at 30%.
  • Skilled vs chart. The skilled pilot plays well; the chart pilot plays the type chart and little else, close to a new player. A hero strong under one and weak under the other is pilot-sensitive, which means a hero that takes skill to play.
  • Not captured. Fielded win% saturates, because every fight fields the whole roster, so it is left out. The sim's move choice rejects some utility signatures (Ink Blast is never taken; Roost Guard is taken 12%, Nevermore 14%), which understates Nautilus, Sentinel and Dread.
